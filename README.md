# My Favorite Movies

## The Green Mile
- **Director:** Frank Darabont  
- **Genre:** Drama / Fantasy  
- **Main Actors:** Tom Hanks, Michael Clarke Duncan  

### About
The Green Mile is about a prison guard who meets a kind inmate with a special power.  
The story shows kindness, miracles, and how unfair life can be sometimes.

---

## Good Will Hunting
- **Director:** Gus Van Sant  
- **Genre:** Drama  
- **Main Actors:** Matt Damon, Robin Williams  

### About
Good Will Hunting is about a young man who is really smart but doesn’t believe in himself.  
With the help of a therapist, he learns to face his past and follow his dreams.
